# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women across India — primarily in rural and semi-urban areas — seeking economic independence. Many are homemakers or informal workers who haven't had access to formal markets or training. They use low-end Android smartphones, may have limited English literacy, and need guidance in their own language. They arrive uncertain about what skills they have and leave with a plan.

## Product Purpose

Shakti is an AI-powered economic empowerment platform that helps women in India identify their marketable skills, learn from achievers, sell in national markets, and access micro-financing — all in their own language. Success looks like a woman going from "I don't know what I can offer" to "I'm earning independently."

## Brand Personality

Warm guide, confident champion, accessible friend. The platform's voice is encouraging without being patronising — like a trusted elder sister who has been through it and knows the way. Not clinical. Not startup-y. Not institutional.

Three words: **warm, confident, grounded**.

## Anti-references

- Clinical government portals: dense forms, cold grays, institutional blue
- Generic SaaS landing pages: centered hero, icon-grid features, corporate blue-on-white, stock-photo empowerment clichés
- Over-designed fashion or luxury aesthetics: Cormorant serif, art-house minimalism that feels cold or exclusive
- Fear-based financial product design: red as primary palette, anxious copy, small print everywhere

## Design Principles

1. **Start with her journey** — Every screen answers "what does she need right now?" not "what does the product want to show?"
2. **Warmth before efficiency** — The platform serves people who may feel like outsiders in formal economies. Warmth and encouragement reduce anxiety and build trust.
3. **Clarity at all literacy levels** — Prose should be plain, actions obvious, feedback immediate. If the interface needs instructions, the interface needs redesign.
4. **Celebrate the ordinary milestone** — Completing a profile, listing a skill, making a first sale are huge moments for this user. The platform acknowledges and celebrates them.
5. **Language is belonging** — Rendering her language correctly, in the right font, is a statement that this platform is for her.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text)
- 14px minimum body text on mobile (many users on low-resolution screens)
- Noto Sans fallbacks for all 8 Indian scripts
- Touch targets minimum 44px
- No color-only communication — always pair with icon or label
- Reduced motion support for vestibular sensitivity
